Hello and welcome to my personal website!

I am a Senior Lecturer in Machine Learning in the Department of Informatics at King's College London, where I head the Knowledge Representation, Reasoning and Multi-Agent Systems research group and am very fortunate to lead the MAASAI research lab (not the Maasai). I previously co-led the Economics and Finance Hub together with Bart de Keijzer and Carmine Ventre, and I am a member of the King's Institute for Artificial Intelligence and an elected member of King's Academic Board.

My research is on artificial intelligence and game theory, particularly multi-agent learning, reinforcement learning and the strategic behaviour of intelligent systems, with applications in financial markets, decentralised finance and cryptoeconomics.

Before joining King's in July 2022, I was a research fellow at the Singapore University of Technology and Design, hosted by Georgios Piliouras. I hold a PhD in game theory, advised by Costis Melolidakis, and an MSc in Statistics and Operations Research from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, a Diploma in Mathematics from TU Darmstadt, and a BSc (Hons) in Business Information Systems from the Athens University of Economics and Business.

For my full list of publications, please see my [CV] or [Google Scholar].

However, if you are interested in research in game theory, multi-agent systems, learning in games, reinforcement learning, AI for finance and/or cryptoeconomics (mechanism design and incentives), write to me about externally or self-funded PhD opportunities. Two projects are currently advertised through the department:

Multi-Agent Learning with Large Language Models
AI and Machine Learning for Financial Markets

See the department's research degrees page for how to apply. When you write, please attach your CV and transcripts and tell me, in a few lines, which problem (or paper of mine) caught your interest and why.

MSc and BSc theses: Students taking the respective project modules can find my thesis topics in the module's project list; they cover similar topics to those above. I supervise around 15 MSc theses each year across the Data Science and Computational Finance MSc programmes, and I welcome industry collaborations and student-proposed projects. For any questions, please email me.
